Renewal Stage Gate — Gate 1 (Advising → OA)

Paste an Opportunity ID, Source_ID, renewal id, or company name. Every verdict is a deterministic field comparison against Salesforce + Snowflake, pulled live. No data is stored in this page.

Accepts a 15/18-char SFDC Opportunity Id, a Source_ID (hawaiian_ice-renewal-#####), a bare renewal id, or a company name. Scope: Benefits Renewal opps in stage Recommendation Sent, Engaged, Alternatives Requested, or ER Confirm.
Gate 1 applies at ER Confirm — the Advising → OA handoff. Before that stage, N/A means the dependent record isn't built yet — not a failure. This tool never asserts a customer is non-compliant; "no registry row" is reported as unknown.
